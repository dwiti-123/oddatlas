- Paris: official live pages returned503. Its official indexed homepage shows EUR31 full rate; the card explicitly discloses the live tariff could not be loaded. No live opening or availability claimed.
- Deep Sleep: former /deep-sleep.asp now404. The new official cabin page confirms GBP399,17hours,14+ and Tanygrisiau. The current experience FAQ confirms meals, backpack/fitness requirements and overnight leaders. Updated old source links; story prose unchanged.
- Skylodge: current page says FEES2026 but the table itself says FEES2025. Do not present its1550/1640PEN as current prices. Ask for a package quote. Overnight transfers, meals and route details are sourced.
- Kolmanskop: the official2026/27 scheduled adult tariff is NAD230. Opening-hour blocks disagree; guide gives tour departures and asks confirmation of gate times. Do not promote photographer permits as standard admission.
- Whichaway: itinerary prices are not stand-alone room rates; select camp and confirm an expedition quote. CapeTown weather buffers are the operator’s recommendation.
- Dhanushkodi: district page carries an old last-update stamp;18km/road access are locator context, not live road or opening confirmation. Return-transport checklist and half-day allowance are editorial.
- Kayakoy: current Ministry museum page lists08:30–18:00, ticket office17:30; no fee inferred from third-party blogs.
