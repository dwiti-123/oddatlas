## What the app can add usefully

Build on the existing story → Plan a visit → source flow. The high-value gap is often a particular decision, not an absent general-purpose feature:

1. Exact stop and directions: distinguish attraction, entrance, departure pier and final viewpoint. One generic pin is not always the destination a traveller needs.
2. How to reach it and return: independent visit versus mandatory guide, public transport where verified, return arrangements, time at the place rather than only total excursion duration.
3. What the advertised price actually buys: per person versus whole vehicle/room, entrance versus transport, mandatory extras and date-specific quotes. Reuse the existing budget calculator when useful; no second calculator is needed.
4. Whether the experience fits the traveller: stairs, climbing, age rules, walking and separate facilities, verified from the operator rather than inferred from photos.
5. What happens if the intended experience changes: operator refund/cancellation terms or a clearly described alternative, where relevant. Hashima already has substantial coverage; do not duplicate it.

A first implementation should improve content and link existing tools rather than add accounts, booking infrastructure, generic AI itineraries or unrelated utility pages. Any new common UI block needs a separate discussion. Existing related-place links are thematic; do not present them as nearby driving stops.

## Dhanushkodi: recommended article intent

Primary problem: I am in Rameswaram and want to see Dhanushkodi's ruins; which stops are different, how can I get there and return, and which published costs can I rely on?

Provisional title: **Dhanushkodi from Rameswaram: Ruins, Route & Visit Plan**.

Candidate query cluster: Rameswaram to Dhanushkodi; how to reach Dhanushkodi from Rameswaram; Dhanushkodi church ruins; Dhanushkodi and Arichal Munai; Dhanushkodi bus or taxi. These are intent candidates, not verified volume estimates. Do not promise bus fares, last-bus times or a cheap fixed taxi fare in the title until resolved.

Existing /places/dhanushkodi should remain the main history/story page. A single practical guide can cover this journey intent and link back to the story. No separate page for each spelling, bus question, lighthouse or ticket question. No application route has been created.

## Proposed guide structure

1. Short story paragraph: ruined coastal settlement and 1964 cyclone, with full-story link.
2. Fast planning answer: start in Rameswaram; choose the church/old settlement, lighthouse and/or Arichal Munai deliberately; arrange the return. A half-day allowance is editorial advice, not an official duration.
3. A small stop table: what each stop is, what visitors can see, what admission/access is still unverified, and a correctly labelled directions link. Verify individual pins before publishing them.
4. Transport comparison: verified public transport details if obtained; independently quoted return vehicle; distinct multi-stop operator package. Avoid substituting a whole-island package for the return-trip fare.
5. Budget: currency/unit, included/excluded costs, season/date limitations and a link to the existing calculator. No fabricated local averages or stale current-price claim.
6. A practical stop sequence with an optional Arichal Munai extension. Treat this as a local outing, not a route between geographically unrelated Odd Atlas places.
7. Brief visitor questions and dated primary/operator sources; keep the longer history on the existing place page.
