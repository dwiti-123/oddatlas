# Odd Atlas topical map

Updated 23 October 2026. Working editorial plan, not a public landing page or a claim of rankings.

## Audience and page roles

Odd Atlas serves curious readers investigating unusual places and travellers checking whether and how they can visit. The same person may have both needs, but each page should have one main job.

- Homepage: introduce the curated collection and route visitors to the appropriate category, story or guide.
- Category: help readers compare relevant places. Do not reproduce full stories.
- Place story: explain the location, documented history, distinguishing features and legends where applicable. Include a brief access overview and point to a visiting guide when one exists.
- Visiting guide: help someone plan the journey using checked sources, clear practical distinctions and explicitly labelled estimates. Briefly summarise history and link to the story for detail.
- Blog index: discover the five existing practical guides. It is not a competing guide to every place.
- Map and random-place tool: discovery and orientation, with links to the canonical stories. They do not replace the stories.
- Saved lists: personal utility, intentionally noindex; not an SEO landing-page target.

## Intended reading paths

Use a small number of descriptive, crawlable links where they help the reader. These are editorial recommendations, not claims that every link below has been implemented.

- Category → relevant story → practical guide where available → story for deeper history.
- Hashima → Kolmanskop: compare settlements sustained by extractive industries.
- Kayaköy → Kuldhara: compare explanations for a community's departure while preserving their different evidence.
- Dhanushkodi → ghost-town category: compare a disaster-damaged settlement with other forms of abandonment. Do not describe those other places as cyclone sites.
- Dolls → haunted-places guide: help readers separate observed displays from supernatural claims.
- Deep Sleep → extraordinary-stays category: compare the journey and setting, not interchangeable rooms or live prices.

Do not add every link to every page, create a giant keyword footer, or rename links to imitate misspelled searches. Maintain existing canonical URLs and recent titles unless evidence establishes a specific problem.
