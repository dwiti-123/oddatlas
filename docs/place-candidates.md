# Odd Atlas — place candidates

## Final selection — 8 October 2026
The user selected Hashima, Dhanushkodi, Kayaköy, Kuldhara, Kolmanskop; Island of the Dolls, Bhangarh Fort, Paris Catacombs, Port Arthur, The Stanley Hotel; Skylodge Adventure Suites, Mirrorcube at Treehotel, Whichaway Camp, Deep Sleep, and Palacio de Sal. The initial stars below are historical recommendations, not the final selection. Treehotel is represented by Mirrorcube alone, without a hotel/room catalogue.

Research date: 2026-10-07. These 30 editorial candidates are not a global popularity ranking or published place records. Stars mark suggested five per category, pending user selection. Paranormal claims are folklore, not established facts. All stays are temporary accommodation, not proof of permanent residence eligibility. Photo rights and complete access guidance remain to be checked before publication.
